4.6 Something Extra: Levels of Abstraction and
Microcode
In computer science, we look at computers and computation at many levels. When using
an application program like a word processing package or a game, we just want its various features to work and we typically do not care how it is written. When we are writing
programs in a high-level language, we tend to view the computer as say, an Ada
machine or a C++ machine, and often do not think about how various language constructs are implemented. The application level and the high-level language level are
two levels of abstraction. As used here, the word “abstraction” can be thought of as
“ignoring the details.”
