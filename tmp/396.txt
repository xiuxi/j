11.2 Packed BCD Instructions
Addition and subtraction operations for packed BCD numbers are similar to those for
multicomponent 2’s complement numbers (Section 4.5). Corresponding bytes of the two
operands are added, and the carry-from-one addition is added to the next pair of bytes.
BCD operands have no special addition instruction; the regular add and adc instructions
are used. However, these instructions are designed for binary values, not BCD values, so
for many operands they give the wrong sums.
The 80x86 architecture includes a daa (decimal adjust after addition) instruction used after an addition instruction to correct the sum. This section explains the operation of the daa instruction and its counterpart das for subtraction. Procedures for
addition and subtraction of non-negative 10-byte packed BCD numbers are developed;
then a general addition procedure is given.
A few examples illustrate the problem with using binary addition for BCD
operands. The AF column gives the value of the auxiliary carry flag, the significance of
which is discussed below.
