250 String Operations
Exercises 7.2
For each exercise below, assume that the data segment contains
source    BYTE   "brown"
dest      BYTE   "brine"
1. Suppose that the following instructions are executed:
lea   esi, source
lea   edi, dest
cld
mov   ecx, 5
repne cmpsb
Assuming that ESI starts at 00010000 and EDI starts at 00010005, what
will be the values stored in ESI and EDI following the repne cmpsb
instruction? What will be stored in ECX?
2. Suppose that the following instructions are executed:
lea   esi, source
lea   edi, dest
cld
mov   ecx, 5
repe cmpsb
Assuming that ESI starts at 00010000 and EDI starts at 00010005, what
will be the values stored in ESI and EDI following the repe cmpsb
instruction? What will be stored in ECX?
