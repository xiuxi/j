Like the add and sub instructions, these instructions are paired with respect to allowable operand types, clock cycles, and bytes of object code. They are summarized
together in Fig. 4.6.
The inc and dec instructions treat the value of the destination operand as an
unsigned integer. They affect the OF, SF, and ZF flags just like addition or subtraction of
one, but they do not change the carry flag CF. Here are examples showing the execution
of a few increment and decrement instructions:
