A calculator isn’t needed to convert a hexadecimal number to its equivalent
binary form. In fact, many binary numbers are too long to be displayed on a typical calculator. Instead, simply substitute four bits for each hex digit. The bits are those found in
the third column of Fig. 1.1, padded with leading zeros as needed. For example,
