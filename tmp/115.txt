As you have seen in this section, the 80x86 architecture includes multiplication
instructions in three formats. You may have noted that the destination of the product
cannot be a memory operand. This may sound restrictive, but some processors have
even greater limitations. In fact, most 8-bit microprocessors, including the Intel 8080, had
no multiplication instruction; any multiplication had to be done using a software routine.
