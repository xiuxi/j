Figure 4.10 summarizes the allowable operand types for mul instructions. No
immediate operand is allowed in a mul. Note the number of clock cycles required is
appreciably larger than for addition or subtraction instructions. The actual number of
clock cycles for the 80386 and 80486 depends on the numbers being multiplied.
