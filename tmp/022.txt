In binary coded decimal (BCD) schemes, each decimal digit is coded with a
string of bits with fixed length, and these strings are pieced together to form the representation. Most frequently four bits are used for each decimal digit; the choices for bit
patterns are shown in Fig. 1.3. Only these ten bit patterns are used.
