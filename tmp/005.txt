The octal (base 8) number system is used with some computer systems. Octal
numbers are written using digits 0 through 7. Most calculators that do hex arithmetic
also handle octal values. It is easy to convert a binary number to octal by writing the
octal equivalent for each group of three bits, or to convert from octal to binary by replacing each octal digit by three bits. To convert from decimal to octal, one can use an algorithm that is the same as the decimal to hex scheme except that you divide by 8 instead
of 16 at each step.
