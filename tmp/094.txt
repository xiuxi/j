Note that you cannot use an xchg instruction to swap two memory operands.
In general, 80x86 instructions do not allow two memory operands.
Like mov instructions, xchg instructions do not alter any status flag; that is,
after execution of an xchg instruction, the contents of the EFLAGS register remains the
same as it was before execution of the instruction.
