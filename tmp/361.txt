Notice that the value 1.2 is shown in Fig. 10.12 as 1.2000000476837158e+0000.
The reason that there are nonzero digits after the decimal point is that 1.2 does not have
an exact representation as a floating point number. The approximation used by the 32bit REAL4 directive translates back to the number shown in 17-decimal-digit precision.
You can get a better approximation by using a REAL8 or a REAL10 directive, but at the
cost of extra bytes of storage.
