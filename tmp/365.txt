Finally we come to a procedure to convert a floating-point parameter to “E notation.” The procedure generates a 12-byte long ASCII string consisting of
• a leading minus sign or a blank
• a digit
• a decimal point
• five digits
• the letter E
• a plus sign or a minus sign
• two digits
This string represents the number in base 10 scientific notation. For example, for the
decimal value 145.8798, the procedure would generate the string b1.45880E+02, where b
represents a blank. Notice that the ASCII string has a rounded value.
